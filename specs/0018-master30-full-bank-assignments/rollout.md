# Rollout and rollback

## Preconditions

Approved staging intent, exact senior-approved full content, complete mappings,
additive migration and default-off flag. Twelve-question evidence is insufficient.

## Staging

Apply task-only migration via advisory-locked runner before code. Check exact
merged SHA flag off, then all-thirty synthetic class/API/browser acceptance with
writing/secrecy/counts/reload/repeats/history and diagnostic safety. Coordinate
shared stage and preserve exact-SHA gate evidence.

## Production

Existing release authorization covers correction. Apply compatible migration via
locked runner, promote exact verified staging SHA, verify frontend/backend SHAs,
then all-thirty synthetic acceptance. Do not touch real learners' assignments.

## Rollback and repair

Disable full-bank creation without deleting answers/versions/attempts. Exposure
safety remains active. Reconcile ledger from persisted attempts without rewriting
legacy scores. Clean only task fixtures after verification, retaining private proof.

## Observability

Stable error/content/attempt IDs without answer text/secrets. Observe blocked
hashes, failed E writes, unavailable/exhausted diagnostics. Receipts preserve
content/deployment hashes, counts and cleanup; credentials never committed.
