`GET /admin/mfa/status` returned `enforce: true`.

Canonical data: `users.role` and `users.is_active` through `/auth/me` and the
admin users list; the session assurance through `/admin/mfa/status`; factors
through Supabase Auth; history through `/admin/users/{id}/security-audit`.
Nothing is optimistic. Every mutation refetches, and a reload must show the
same state.
