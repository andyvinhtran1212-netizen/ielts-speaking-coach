## Specific editorial attention

- Preserve the speaker's level of certainty and whether a figure is a mean,
  median, estimate, correction or final count. Keep denominators and time
  periods attached to the right numbers.
- Keep observations separate from explanations or causal claims. A proposed
  action must not become a completed or approved decision in translation.
- Distinguish reserved from confirmed resources, and preserve dependencies,
  named owners, deadlines and review points.
- Check health, privacy and access statements for their stated limits; avoid
  broadening a qualified claim. Compare wording with the audio and script
