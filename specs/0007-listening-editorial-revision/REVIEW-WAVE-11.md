## Focused wording correction

Eight transfer prompts in batches 58 and 60 previously rendered “Map…” as
“Lập sơ đồ…”. Because the learner answers in text, that wording could imply
an unavailable drawing task. The Vietnamese now asks the learner to
“Trình bày theo bốn mục…”, retaining all four requested relations. The
source English, IDs, rubric and scoring remain untouched. Reviewers should
confirm this interpretation against each form's script and response UI.
