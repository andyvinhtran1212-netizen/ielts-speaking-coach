## Specific editorial attention

- B2.5-096: retain observed association versus causal mechanism, the two
  confounders, the controlled pilot and a bounded conclusion. A recommended
  test must not become a promised result.
- B2.5-097: explain the local expression “a decent first go” in context
  without upgrading the evidence. Ministerial and public registers may
  differ, but the caution and limits must remain. Confirm what the new audio
  has not yet measured.
- B2.5-098: keep the initial owner and deadline as distractors separate
  from the final accepted owner and revised deadline. Preserve why the date
  changed and what the intermediate Wednesday means.
- B2.5-099: distinguish recommendation, condition before approval,
  limited regional evidence and final decision. Rhetorical compression must
  not erase the population limit or turn a provisional step into approval.
- B2.5-100: preserve the strength of evidence, independent review, coverage
  question, provisional funding status and later review basis. Keep the
  corrected visit date separate from its distractor.
