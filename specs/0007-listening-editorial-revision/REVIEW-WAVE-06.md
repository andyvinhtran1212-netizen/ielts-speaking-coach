## Specific editorial attention

- B2.1-096: preserve experimental versus comparison groups, observed
  productivity, selection effects and the explicit limit on causal inference.
  For transfer questions, keep measured attendance or overtime distinct from
  learning or schedule effects that were not established.
- B2.1-097: retain **mean** versus **median**, the unit, period and measured
  population. Separate a headline claim from measured evidence; keep price
  change, coverage limits and requested follow-up checks distinct.
- B2.1-098: distinguish a proposed pilot, conditional approvals, the start
  date, later review and possible permanent change. Preserve the order and
  number of deadlines, visits or budget steps in transfer tasks.
- B2.1-099: distinguish measured centre visits, survey-reported views and
  climate projections. Higher attendance does not itself demonstrate a
  health benefit; keep the staged funding decision conditional.
- B2.1-100: preserve the tested site scope, unmeasured long-term health
  outcome, first-quarter versus later costs, projection conditions and all
  three checks before wider implementation. The summary and exam questions
  explicitly limit what receives credit; translation must not add a criterion.
