# Reviewed Part 1 prompts

Each source ID retains the date window from the supplied document. These are authored practice questions, not verified live IELTS questions.

## p1-001
- Do you work, or are you a student?
- What do you enjoy most about your work or studies?
- What is your workplace or place of study like?
- Would you like to change anything about your work or studies?

## p1-002
- Where is your hometown?
- What do you like most about your hometown?
- Has your hometown changed much in recent years?
- Would you like to live there in the future?

## p1-003
- Do you live in a house or an apartment?
- Who do you live with?
- Which room do you spend the most time in?
- Is there anything you would like to change about your home?

## p1-004
- Do you often visit street markets?
- What do you usually buy at a street market?
- Did you visit street markets when you were a child?
- What do you like most about street markets?

## p1-005
- Do you enjoy travelling?
- Where do you usually go when you travel?
- Did you travel much when you were younger?
- Do you prefer travelling alone or with other people?

## p1-006
- Have you ever collected anything?
- What kinds of things do people collect in your country?
- Did you collect anything as a child?
- Would you like to start a collection in the future?

## p1-007
- How often do you use a computer or tablet?
- What do you mainly use it for?
- Did you use computers much when you were a child?
- Do you prefer using a computer or a tablet?

## p1-008
- What kind of shoes do you usually wear?
- How often do you buy new shoes?
- Do you choose shoes for comfort or appearance?
- Have your preferences in shoes changed over time?

## p1-009
- Do you recycle anything at home?
- Is it easy to recycle in the area where you live?
- Did you learn about recycling at school?
- Would you like to change any of your habits around waste?

## p1-010
- What is considered polite in your country?
- Who taught you how to be polite?
- Are you more careful about politeness in formal situations?
- Has your idea of politeness changed as you have grown older?

## p1-011
- Does your name have a special meaning?
- Do you like your name?
- Is your name common where you live?
- Are you good at remembering other people's names?

## p1-012
- When do you usually feel bored?
- What do you do when you are bored?
- Were you often bored as a child?
- Can boredom sometimes lead you to try something new?

## p1-013
- Is there a teacher you remember well?
- What qualities do you appreciate in a teacher?
- Did a teacher ever encourage you to learn something new?
- Would you like to teach someone a skill one day?

## p1-014

- How often do you use social media?
- What do you usually do on social media?
- Has the way you use social media changed over time?

## p1-015

- Do you prefer to share your ambitions with other people?

## p1-016
